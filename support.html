How to use Kamilia

? Common questions

Is my data on the cloud?
Solo cycle logs stay on your device. Companion is optional: Sign in with Apple and a limited season snapshot if you share with your husband. Full diary dump is not uploaded.
How do I export my data?
Open the You tab → Export my data. Kamilia writes a JSON file and opens the system share sheet so you can save or send a copy.
How do I delete everything?
You → Delete everything. Confirm once. Logs, cycle state, and preferences are permanently removed from this device.
Why are fertile days an estimate?
Because bodies vary. Kamilia shows a window with confidence language and separates what you logged from what is predicted. It never claims contraception or “safe days.”
Can Kamilia give a fatwa?
No. Madhab selection only tailors educational copy and soft rules. Confirm important purity questions with a scholar you trust.
Reminders are not showing
You → Reminders. Turn a toggle on (this may ask for notification permission). If you denied earlier, open iOS Settings → Kamilia → Notifications.
Face ID lock
You → Face ID lock. Enabling requires a successful biometric check. Backgrounding the app re-locks when lock is on.

i For App Review

No login required. All features work offline after onboarding. Demo path: complete onboarding → Today → Log → You → Privacy policy / Export / Delete. External services: none in this version.
