Privacy policy

Local first. Companion optional. You choose what to share.

Updated 21 October 2026

The short version

  • ✓ No account required for solo cycle logging
  • ✓ Cycle logs and preferences stay on this device first
  • ✓ Companion is optional: Sign in with Apple + limited season snapshot to one husband
  • ✓ We do not sell data or use advertising identifiers
  • ✓ You can export a JSON copy or delete everything in the app
  • ✓ Face ID / notifications only if you choose them

1 Who we are

Kamilia is a personal cycle and ritual-purity companion for iOS. It helps married, practicing Muslim women mark ḥayḍ (menstruation) and ṭuhr (purity), keep worship in view, and see fertility signals as honest estimates.

This product is local-first for your diary. Solo use does not require an account. Companion is optional: if you choose Share with husband, Sign in with Apple creates a limited session and may sync a reduced season snapshot to one companion — not your full diary. Contact: support@kamilia.app.

2 What we store on your device

Core data is stored on your iPhone or iPad using Apple’s on-device storage (SwiftData / UserDefaults) under iOS data protection when the device is locked.

Cycle & daily logs
Flow level, optional symptoms, temperature, mucus notes, intimacy flag, and free-text notes you choose to write. Cycle bookkeeping (period start, purity state, ghusl prompt, recent cycle lengths).
Preferences
School of law (madhab), intent preference, qaḍāʾ make-up fast count, onboarding completion, reminder toggles, Face ID lock preference.
Biometrics
We never store Face ID or Touch ID templates. Unlock is handled entirely by Apple’s LocalAuthentication framework when you enable app lock.
What we do not store for ads
Advertising ID, location history, contacts, photos from your library, HealthKit samples, or payment credentials for core solo use.

3 What may leave your device

Solo logging
Solo cycle logging does not upload your diary to our servers. If you share an export file (You → Export my data), that share is under your control via the iOS share sheet.
Companion (optional)
If you opt into Companion, the app may send a reduced season snapshot and care preferences you enable to our Companion backend (Supabase), visible only to the husband you link. A device token may be registered for optional Companion alerts when that delivery path is available. Full symptom diaries, intimacy notes, and free text are not part of the default Companion snapshot. You can pause or remove sharing anytime.
Subscriptions (optional)
Apple handles every payment. To show the plans and to confirm a subscription on your devices, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your App Store purchase and renewal records if you have any, basic device details such as the iOS version and, like any server, your IP address. RevenueCat never receives your cycle logs, your notes or anything you track. We switch off its advertising identifier collection and its diagnostics.

We do not sell personal data. We do not send cycle data to advertising or AI training services in this version.

4 Health & sensitive data

Menstrual and purity-related logs are sensitive. On device they use iOS data protection when your phone is locked. Companion share is scoped to toggles you enable. For App Store privacy labels we treat health-related logs as used for app functionality; Companion links a limited identity only if you Sign in with Apple for that feature.

Not medical advice
Predictions are estimates with confidence language. Kamilia is a wellness and lifestyle companion, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or provide contraception advice.

5 Permissions

Face ID / Touch ID
Only if you turn on app lock under You. Used solely to unlock Kamilia on this device.
Notifications
Only if you allow them. Used for optional period-near estimates, evening log nudges, and (when available) Companion alerts. Never for marketing. You can turn reminders off anytime.
Sign in with Apple
Only if you open Companion. Creates a limited session for invite, share, and snapshot sync — not required for solo logging.
Not requested for core solo use
Location, camera, microphone, photo library, HealthKit, Bluetooth, tracking (ATT).

6 Your controls

  • Export

    You → Export my data creates a JSON file on device (no passcode included).

  • Delete everything

    You → Delete everything permanently clears logs, cycle state, and preferences on this device.

  • App lock

    Optional Face ID / Touch ID so others with your phone cannot open the app.

  • Companion pause / remove

    You → Companion: pause or remove sharing anytime. He cannot expand what you share.

7 Children

Kamilia is not directed at children under 13. It is intended for adult women managing their own cycle and worship.

8 Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above, the in-app privacy text, and (when applicable) App Store privacy labels. Material changes that involve new data leaving the device will be clear before you upgrade into them.
